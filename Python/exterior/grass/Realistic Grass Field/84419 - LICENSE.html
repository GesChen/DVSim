About "Realistic Grass Field":

  • Blender: 2.76
  • Render Engine: Cycles
  • Uploaded on: 2016-05-14 14:41:55
Hey guys! Thanks for checking out my grass scene.This will be my first upload to Blendswap. I'vewatched plenty of tutorials on grass over the years but this time I decided to do an environmenton my own without help. I am sure you will see some techniques I use that you can pinpoint to exactly where I learned it from. Anyway! I tried to keep this file clean. Everythingis named appropriately. Right now the ground plane you see has 7 particle systems (one for each plant).They are all hidden from view because they slowmy computer down. I also cut the ground planeto fit the camera to save render time. But you caneasily go into edit mode and modify the ground to be whatever size you desire.No external textures or models were used! I modeled everything and setup all of the materials manually myself. Everything is setup in blender cycles. All of the plant modelsare located on the last layer. Also notice I am using volumetric lighting which slows down renders a lot.Also if you edit the ground plane be sure to checkout the vertex painting I have done for eachparticle system. You can edit the vertex paint toeasily change the density and length of plants.This took me a little over a week to finish. I hopeyou like it and I would love to hear anything youhave to say about it (: If you use it I would love to hear about it! Just PM me here. Thanks Guys!
